A label will not scan

Place the package on a steady, evenly lit surface. Avoid glare, keep the whole panel in frame, and provide both Ingredients and Nutrition. The Front photo is optional. Check your connection before trying again.

A value looks wrong

Correct uncertain fields on the review screen before saving or comparing. Always check the original package, especially for allergens, medical diets, and formulation changes.

I cannot compare two products

Both products need a compatible nutrition basis and should be from a comparable category. Rescan a missing panel or correct the serving amount when prompted.

Where is my history?

History is stored only on your device. LabelFit has no account or cloud sync. Deleting the app or restoring without its app data may remove your history.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual items from History, or open Settings and delete all local data. LabelFit does not keep a server-side account or analysis history.

Does LabelFit give allergy or medical advice?

No. LabelFit is informational and automated extraction can be wrong. It cannot determine whether a product is safe. Always read the package and ask a qualified professional when appropriate.
