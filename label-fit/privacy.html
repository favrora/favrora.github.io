Privacy Policy

LabelFit is becoming CaloriePad. If your installed app is named CaloriePad, read the CaloriePad Privacy Policy. The policy below applies to earlier LabelFit versions.

No account, no advertising, no cross-app tracking. Your saved products and comparisons stay on your device.

Who operates LabelFit

LabelFit is developed and operated by David Voskanyan (“LabelFit,” “we,” “us,” or “our”).

Information processed for an analysis

When you tap Analyze, LabelFit may process one to three package photos, your selected goal and ingredient terms to flag, a pseudonymous billing identifier, a request identifier, the app version, subscription entitlement and remaining allowance, a security attestation token, and technical request information needed for security, rate limiting, reliability, and fraud prevention.

Do not include people, payment cards, prescriptions, addresses, or other sensitive personal information in package photos.

How information is used

We use this information to authenticate LabelFit, return the requested analysis or comparison, manage allowances, validate subscriptions, prevent abuse, operate and improve the service, and comply with applicable law. We do not use it for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Local data

Analyses, comparisons, preferences, and favorites are stored in LabelFit's private storage on your device. Package photos are saved locally only if you enable that option. Delete individual items from History or erase all local data in Settings.

Server and provider retention

LabelFit's application server does not persist package photos, extracted label content, goals, avoid terms, or analysis results. Minimal operational logs exclude photo bytes, prompt text, extracted ingredients, nutrition values, and AI responses.

Google Gemini API reads submitted label photos and request text. We use a paid API project. Google states that paid prompts and responses are not used to improve its products, but it may log them for a limited period solely for safety, abuse monitoring, and required legal disclosures.

Firebase App Check with Apple App Attest on iOS and Play Integrity on Android verifies authentic app requests. Attestation material is processed for validation. Firebase states that limited-use App Check tokens used for replay protection may be stored for up to 30 days.

RevenueCat validates App Store and Google Play subscription receipts, restores eligible purchases, and checks whether LabelFit Pro is active. It processes a pseudonymous app user identifier, product and purchase status, storefront, and related technical events. LabelFit does not send label photos or extracted label content to RevenueCat.

Apple App Store and Google Play process purchases, renewals, cancellations, refunds, taxes, and payment information under their own privacy terms. LabelFit does not receive full payment card details.

Firebase Analytics processes pseudonymous usage events such as analyses started or completed, comparisons, paywall views, and purchase outcomes. We use these events to understand usage and improve reliability. LabelFit does not send label photos, extracted nutrition values, or user-entered ingredient terms in analytics events.

Cloud hosting and network providers process request metadata to deliver and protect the service. Information may be processed where these providers operate under applicable contractual and legal protections.

Retention and deletion

Local content remains until you delete it or remove the app. LabelFit has no server-side account or analysis history. Hashed quota records and RevenueCat purchase records may be retained to enforce allowances, restore purchases, prevent abuse, and meet financial or legal obligations. Operational security logs are retained only as reasonably needed for service integrity, incident response, and legal obligations. Provider retention follows the practices described above.

Age requirement

LabelFit requires users to be 18 or older because online analysis uses Google Gemini. The 9+ content rating displayed in the U.S. App Store describes the app’s content. LabelFit is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Food, allergy, and medical information

LabelFit provides general informational assistance. It is not medical or dietary advice and cannot determine whether a product is safe. Always verify the original package.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to personal-information processing. Most user-controlled information can be viewed or deleted in the app. Because LabelFit has no account and does not attach requests to your name, we generally cannot locate a past provider request by name or email.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app, providers, or law changes. We will update the effective date and provide additional notice when required.
