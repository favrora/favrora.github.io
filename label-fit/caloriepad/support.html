How do I log a meal?

Tap Add meal on Today. Take a photo, describe the meal, search for a food, scan a package barcode, or enter the food manually. Check the portions and nutrition, then save the meal.

What if an estimate or lookup fails?

Make sure you have a connection, then retry. Use a clear photo with the entire meal or nutrition panel in view and avoid glare. If a barcode has no match or the service is unavailable, enter the information from the package manually. Edit any result that differs from what you ate.

Where are my meals and saved products?

Open History to review your meals or find products saved in LabelFit on this device. CaloriePad keeps your diary on the device and does not sync it to other devices. Uninstalling the app can remove the diary.

How do I restore or manage my subscription?

Sign in to the store account that made the purchase. Open CaloriePad Pro and tap Restore purchases. To manage, cancel, or check the renewal date, open Apple subscriptions on iPhone or Google Play subscriptions on Android. An existing subscription may be listed as LabelFit in the store.

How do I delete my data?

Open a meal in History to delete it, or choose Delete all local data in Settings to erase your saved app content on this device. For help with online request data, read the Privacy Policy.

Where do the nutrition values come from?

Food search uses USDA FoodData Central. Packaged product lookups use data from Open Food Facts, available under the Open Database License. Photos and descriptions can help estimate portions or read a label. Check the original packaging and adjust the amounts before relying on the result.
