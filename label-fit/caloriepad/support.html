How do I log a meal?

Tap Take a photo on Today. Use the camera or choose a photo from your library. Check the estimated foods and portions before saving. If you cannot use the camera or the estimate is unavailable, use manual entry.

What if a photo estimate fails?

Use a clear photo with the whole meal in view. Check your connection and retry, or enter the meal manually. Adjust the ingredients and portions if they differ from what you ate.

Where are my meals and saved products?

Open History and choose a date to review your meals. In Profile, you can set a daily calorie goal and find products previously saved in LabelFit. CaloriePad keeps your diary on the device and does not sync it to other devices. Uninstalling the app can remove the diary.

How do I restore or manage my subscription?

Sign in to the store account that made the purchase. Open CaloriePad Pro and tap Restore purchases. To manage, cancel, or check the renewal date, open Apple subscriptions on iPhone or Google Play subscriptions on Android. An existing subscription may be listed as LabelFit in the store.

How do I delete my data?

Open a meal in History to delete it, or choose Delete all local data in Settings to erase your saved app content on this device. For help with online request data, read the Privacy Policy.

Where do the nutrition values come from?

The meal estimate uses nutrition data from USDA FoodData Central.
