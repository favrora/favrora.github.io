Privacy Policy

The guest diary stays on your device. If you choose to sign in when account access is available, CaloriePad backs up your account diary to its dedicated service. Online meal estimates send the information needed to complete the request.

Who operates CaloriePad

David Voskanyan operates CaloriePad, the successor to LabelFit.

Diary and device permissions

The app stores guest and account diaries separately on your device. Account sync stores meals, templates, preferences, and nutrition goal inputs in a separate database. Saved LabelFit history and in-progress capture drafts stay on your device. An unfinished photo is kept in app storage so you can resume the draft. Saving or discarding a draft removes its temporary photo. If you import your guest diary, a copy of those entries joins your account. Manual entries and saved diary records are available offline. CaloriePad uses the camera to take meal photos and accesses photos you select for analysis. If you skip these permissions, you can still log food manually. Photos you select remain in your library.

Account sign-in and deletion

For an optional account, Firebase Authentication processes your email address, provider identity and security information when you sign in with Apple, Google or email. Your account identifier separates your diary from other accounts. You can request account deletion in Profile → Account after confirming your sign-in. Deletion removes your synced diary and account. It leaves your guest diary and store subscription available for separate management. The deletion page is linked from Account when sign-in is enabled.

Online meal estimates

When you request an online estimate, the CaloriePad service receives the meal photo you submit and the app language. Google Gemini processes this content to identify foods and estimate portions. Google describes how it handles paid API requests, including limited logging for safety and legal obligations, in the Gemini API terms.

A food name inferred from your photo may be used to search USDA FoodData Central. Please submit only food or package information and avoid including faces, addresses, or other personal details.

Security, billing, and analytics

  • Firebase App Check verifies requests using Apple or Google device attestation. We use pseudonymous installation and billing identifiers, request identifiers, and technical request data to authenticate requests, manage allowances, and prevent abuse.
  • Apple and Google handle payment, renewal, cancellation, and refunds for their stores. RevenueCat processes a pseudonymous billing identifier and transaction data to verify or restore purchases. We do not receive full payment card details.
  • With your permission, Amplitude receives a randomly generated installation identifier, app version, build, platform, and event timestamps. Usage events include app opens, screen visits, onboarding, meal saves and paywall actions, including the plan and result of a purchase or restore where applicable. Diagnostics include the time a photo estimate takes and a limited set of error codes. Events do not include photos, diary text, nutrition values, account identifiers, address fields, or screen recordings. We use these events to understand usage and improve the app. The app sends a placeholder IP value to prevent location enrichment from the connection IP. Amplitude can still see the IP address of the network connection.

Retention and deletion

Usage analytics is optional. You can allow it when the app asks or in Profile. Turn off Usage analytics there at any time to stop new events. If you turn it back on, the app uses a new identifier. The app keeps earlier identifiers on this installation so support can find events already sent. To request deletion of those events, use Request analytics deletion in Profile. It opens an email with the identifiers for you to send to support. If you have already removed the app or cleared its data, contact support for help.

The CaloriePad estimate service does not write original photos or submitted descriptions to its request database. Food names in diary entries you choose to sync are part of your account diary. To recover from a dropped connection, it stores an encrypted copy of the generated estimate. Retrying the same request can retrieve it for up to 15 minutes. The service schedules that copy for deletion 24 hours after completion.

A separate record with a hashed request identifier and completion status prevents retries from counting twice against the analysis allowance. The service schedules that record for deletion after 70 days and allowance records for deletion 70 days after their last update. Database deletion runs asynchronously. Cloud hosting may retain technical logs for service operations and security. These logs are designed to omit request content. Providers apply their own retention practices.

To erase the diary and saved app content on this device, open Settings from Profile and choose Delete all local data. Device backups and original library photos are managed through your operating system. Deleting local data does not cancel a subscription or delete store transaction records.

Age and privacy requests

CaloriePad is intended for people 18 and older. If you have questions about this policy or want to exercise privacy rights available where you live, email webcoder.ru@gmail.com. If you use CaloriePad without an account, we may need additional information to identify records associated with your request. Avoid sending meal photos or medical information by email.
