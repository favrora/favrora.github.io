Privacy Policy

CaloriePad has no sign-in or cloud diary. Your saved meals, nutrition goals, and templates are stored on your device. Online meal estimates send the information needed to complete the request.

Who operates CaloriePad

David Voskanyan operates CaloriePad, the successor to LabelFit.

Diary and device permissions

We store your diary, goals, templates, preferences, and any saved LabelFit history in the app's storage on your device. If you estimate your daily goal, your age, height, weight, activity level, and formula reference stay on your device. Manual entries and saved diary records are available offline. CaloriePad uses the camera to take meal photos, and accesses photos you select for analysis. If you skip these permissions, you can still log food manually. Photos that you choose from your library remain in your library.

Online meal estimates

When you request an online estimate, the CaloriePad service receives the meal photo you submit and the app language. Google Gemini processes this content to identify foods and estimate portions. Google describes how it handles paid API requests, including limited logging for safety and legal obligations, in the Gemini API terms.

A food name inferred from your photo may be used to search USDA FoodData Central. Please submit only food or package information and avoid including faces, addresses, or other personal details.

Security, billing, and analytics

  • Firebase App Check verifies requests using Apple or Google device attestation. We use pseudonymous installation and billing identifiers, request identifiers, and technical request data to authenticate requests, manage allowances, and prevent abuse.
  • Apple and Google handle payment, renewal, cancellation, and refunds for their stores. RevenueCat processes a pseudonymous billing identifier and transaction data to verify or restore purchases. We do not receive full payment card details.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase processes pseudonymous app and device data and usage events, such as an estimate finishing or a purchase failing. We use these signals to understand usage and improve reliability. Our analytics events omit submitted photos, food descriptions, nutrition values, and diary records.

Retention and deletion

The CaloriePad service does not write original photos or food descriptions to its database. To recover from a dropped connection, it stores an encrypted copy of the estimate that can be retrieved by retrying the same request for up to 15 minutes. That record is scheduled for database deletion after 24 hours. Deletion runs asynchronously.

Hashed allowance records are scheduled for deletion 70 days after their last update. Cloud hosting may retain technical logs for service operations and security. These logs are designed to omit request content. Providers apply their own retention practices.

To erase the diary and saved app content on this device, open Settings from Profile and choose Delete all local data. Device backups and original library photos are managed through your operating system. Deleting local data does not cancel a subscription or delete store transaction records.

Age and privacy requests

CaloriePad is intended for people 18 and older. If you have questions about this policy or want to exercise privacy rights available where you live, email webcoder.ru@gmail.com. Because CaloriePad has no account, we may need additional information to identify records associated with your request. Avoid sending meal photos or medical information by email.
