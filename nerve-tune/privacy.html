Privacy Policy

No account, no advertising, no cross-app tracking. Your check-ins, practice history, outcomes, favorites and insights stay on your device.

Who operates Nervo

Nervo was previously named NerveTune and is developed and operated by David Voskanyan (“Nervo,” “we,” “us,” or “our”).

Local wellness data

Nervo stores your selected state, practice context, completed practices, ratings, favorites, preferences and calculated insights in the app’s private storage on your device. This information is used locally to select and improve future recommendations. It is not uploaded to us or RevenueCat.

You can erase check-ins, sessions, favorites and preferences from Settings. Nervo also lets you export selected local practice data to a password-encrypted file. You choose where that file is saved or shared; Nervo does not upload it. Removing the app also removes its local database. Deleting local app data does not cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription.

Subscriptions

Apple or Google processes store purchases, billing, refunds and subscription management under the privacy practices of the store you use. RevenueCat processes a random app-user identifier, product identifiers, purchase status, renewal and expiration information, and limited device/app technical information so Nervo can verify access to Pro features and restore purchases. Nervo does not provide RevenueCat with your name, email, wellness check-ins, practice history or ratings.

Notifications

If you enable a reminder, its schedule and neutral message are stored on your device and delivered through the device’s local notification system. Nervo does not use a server to send reminders.

Diagnostics and analytics

Nervo does not use advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking. RevenueCat may produce aggregate purchase and subscription metrics for us to understand subscription performance; these metrics do not include wellness data. In a build configured with Google Analytics for Firebase, an adult user can choose in Settings to share usage analytics. After that opt-in, Google Analytics may process an app-instance identifier, app and device information, approximate location derived from IP, interactions, and automatic session or store-purchase events. Nervo does not send your check-ins, individual practice history, ratings, searches, age selection or RevenueCat identifier to Google Analytics. We use this opt-in analytics to improve Nervo, without advertising or cross-app tracking. You can stop future collection in Settings at any time; this does not delete events already received by Google. Google Analytics is unavailable in on-device mode and in builds not configured with it. Apple or Google may also provide developers with aggregated store and diagnostic information according to your device and store privacy choices.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information. We do not share wellness data for advertising, tracking, data-broker profiling or automated medical decisions. The applicable app store and RevenueCat handle subscription verification and entitlement management. RevenueCat may use subscription information for aggregate purchase and subscription analytics, security, support and legal compliance. In a build configured with Google Analytics for Firebase, Google processes the opt-in analytics data described above for product analytics, not advertising or cross-app tracking.

Retention

Local data remains until you delete it or remove the app. RevenueCat retains purchase records as needed to provide subscription entitlements, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations. Apple or Google controls retention of its store transaction records.

Age and on-device mode

Versions without the age range screen are for people aged 18 or older. In versions with the age range screen, people aged 9–17 can use the free on-device mode. Nervo stores only the resulting mode on your device and does not send your age selection. The on-device mode does not connect to RevenueCat, start or restore purchases, or request an app store review. Practices, wellness history and optional reminders are handled on your device. Your operating system may back up eligible app data according to your settings.

If you choose to open our online legal or support pages, your browser connects to that website. If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian to contact us for support.

Wellness, not medical care

Nervo is a general wellness tool. It is not medical care and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. Stop a practice if you experience pain, strong dizziness, breathing difficulty or other discomfort. In an emergency, contact local emergency services.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning personal information. Most Nervo information can be viewed or erased directly in the app. Because there is no account and local wellness information is not sent to us, we generally cannot locate that information by name or email.

Data deletion requests

Delete local wellness data at any time from Nervo Settings. For a privacy request concerning subscription-support records processed for Nervo, email team@beautyai.app with the subject “Nervo data deletion.” Apple, Google and RevenueCat may retain transaction records where required for billing, fraud prevention or legal compliance.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app, providers or law changes. We will update the effective date and provide additional notice when required.
