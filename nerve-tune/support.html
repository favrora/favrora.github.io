A practice feels uncomfortable

Stop the practice. NerveTune should never require forced breathing, breath holds or painful movement. Return to normal breathing and your surroundings. Seek qualified care for health concerns and local emergency services in an emergency.

My history or insights are missing

History is stored only on your device. NerveTune has no account or cloud sync. Deleting the app or restoring an iPhone without its app data may remove local history.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings in NerveTune and choose “Delete all local data.” This removes check-ins, sessions, favorites and preferences. It does not cancel a subscription.

How do I cancel or manage Pro?

On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions and NerveTune. Apple manages billing, cancellations and refund requests. You can also use “Manage subscription” inside NerveTune.

How do I restore a purchase?

Confirm you are signed in with the Apple ID used for the purchase, then open NerveTune Settings or the Pro screen and tap “Restore purchases.”

Does NerveTune provide medical advice?

No. NerveTune is a general wellness tool, not medical or mental-health care, diagnosis, therapy or crisis support.
